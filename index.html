Guidelines & FAQs

Stay Policies & Information

Important check-in guidelines and common queries regarding society accommodation.

Standard Check-in is at 12:00 PM (Noon) and Check-out is at 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out is subject to room availability and prior coordination with the desk manager.
Priority accommodation is given to members of the Wayanad District Police Co-operative Society, Kerala Police fraternity personnel, visiting government officers, and their families. Group bookings for visiting official delegations and tour parties are also facilitated.
All adult guests must produce a valid government photo ID (Kerala Police Department ID Card, PEN card, Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Voter ID). Police personnel should provide their unit/station details for official register entry.
Tariffs can be remitted directly at the society office or through UPI / Bank transfer. Official society receipts are issued for all payments. Member-subsidized rates apply upon presentation of society membership verification.
