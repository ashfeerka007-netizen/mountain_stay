## 🏔️ Overview

**Mountain Stay Retreat** provides safe, clean, and comfortable lodging and dormitory accommodations in Kalpetta, Wayanad for:
- Kerala Police Society members and fraternity officers
- Visiting government officials & duty batches
- Member families on vacation in Wayanad
- Official delegations & tour groups

---

## 🛏️ Accommodation Features

- **Executive Suite Rooms:** Air-conditioned master double cot rooms with attached modern bathrooms and sofa lounge.
- **Deluxe Family Rooms:** Spacious multi-bed rooms designed for families visiting Wayanad hill stations.
- **Fraternity Dormitory Halls:** Bright, ventilated shared dormitory halls with clean individual cot beds, personal lockers, and hygienic washroom blocks.
- **Direct WhatsApp Reservation Desk:** Instant reservation request generator linking directly to the society accommodation desk (`+91 8301995940`).
- **Interactive Wayanad Sightseeing Guide:** Proximity and travel duration estimates to Pookode Lake, Chembra Peak, Banasura Sagar Dam, and Edakkal Caves.
